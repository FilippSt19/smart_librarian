## Title: The Hobbit

Author: J.R.R. Tolkien

Genre: Fantasy

Themes: Friendship, Adventure, Courage, Personal Growth

Summary:
Bilbo Baggins, a quiet hobbit, joins a group of dwarves on a quest to reclaim their homeland from the dragon Smaug. Along the journey, he discovers bravery, loyalty, and confidence while facing trolls, goblins, elves, and other magical creatures.

------------------------------------------------------------

## Title: Harry Potter and the Philosopher's Stone

Author: J.K. Rowling

Genre: Fantasy

Themes: Magic, Friendship, Good vs Evil

Summary:
Harry Potter discovers that he is a wizard and begins studying at Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry. Alongside his friends Ron and Hermione, he uncovers secrets about his past and confronts the dark wizard Voldemort.

## Title: 1984

Author: George Orwell

Genre: Dystopian Fiction

Themes: Freedom, Surveillance, Totalitarianism

## Title: Animal Farm

Author: George Orwell

Genre: Political Satire

Themes: Power, Corruption, Equality

Summary:
Farm animals overthrow their human owner hoping to create a fair society. However, the pigs gradually become just as oppressive as the humans, illustrating how power can corrupt even noble ideals.
